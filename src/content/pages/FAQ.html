Solar FAQ Ontario

ONTARIO SOLAR
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to common questions about commercial solar incentives, residential solar panels, battery storage, eligibility, rebates, and solar installation in Toronto and across Ontario.

What commercial solar incentives are available in Ontario?
+
Ontario businesses may have access to commercial solar incentives, energy-efficiency rebates, clean technology tax credits, and funding programs. Availability depends on project type, system design, utility requirements, program rules, timing, and approval. Army Energy helps businesses review solar incentive pathways before installation.
Do I need approval before installing commercial solar panels?
+
Some Ontario solar incentive programs require pre-approval before installation begins. If work starts before approval, the project may lose eligibility. Businesses should confirm all program requirements before committing to a commercial solar installation.
Does Army Energy install commercial solar panels in Toronto?
+
Yes. Army Energy provides commercial solar installation support in Toronto, the GTA, and across Ontario for warehouses, industrial buildings, farms, greenhouses, retail plazas, office buildings, and institutional properties.
What types of properties are best for commercial solar?
+
Commercial solar is often a strong fit for properties with large rooftops, open land, high daytime electricity usage, and long-term ownership plans. Warehouses, factories, farms, greenhouses, retail plazas, offices, and institutions can all be good candidates.
Can farms and greenhouses qualify for solar incentives?
+
Farms, greenhouses, and agricultural operations may qualify for commercial solar incentives or energy-efficiency programs depending on utility account type, system size, project design, and current program rules. Eligibility should be reviewed before installation.
Can residential homeowners get solar rebates in Ontario?
+
Ontario homeowners may have access to residential solar rebates, battery storage incentives, or home energy upgrade programs depending on current availability and eligibility. Army Energy can help homeowners understand residential solar options in Toronto and Ontario.
Can solar panels reduce my hydro bill?
+
Yes. A properly designed solar energy system can offset part of your electricity usage and help reduce long-term hydro costs. Actual savings depend on system size, electricity usage, utility rates, roof orientation, shading, and available incentives.
Can battery storage be added to a solar system?
+
Yes. Battery storage can be added to many commercial and residential solar systems to support backup power, energy resilience, peak demand management, and smarter energy use. The best setup depends on your property and electrical needs.
What does behind-the-meter solar mean?
+
Behind-the-meter solar means the solar system is installed on your property and primarily serves your building’s electricity usage. It helps reduce the electricity purchased from the grid by generating power on-site.
Is solar incentive funding guaranteed?
+
No. Solar incentive funding is not guaranteed. Rebates and incentives are subject to eligibility, program approval, documentation, funding availability, and changing program rules. Always confirm current details before relying on incentive support.
