Is residential solar worth it in Ontario?

Residential solar may be worth it for homeowners with suitable roof space, strong electricity usage, and long-term plans to stay in the home.

Yes. Army Energy supports residential solar panel installation in Toronto, the GTA, and across Ontario.

Yes. Battery storage can be added to many residential solar systems to improve backup power and energy flexibility.
