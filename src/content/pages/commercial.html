Commercial Solar FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About
Commercial Solar in Ontario

Is commercial solar worth it in Ontario?

Commercial solar can be worth it for businesses with strong daytime electricity usage, suitable rooftop or ground space, and long-term ownership plans.

What types of businesses can use commercial solar?

Warehouses, factories, farms, greenhouses, retail plazas, office buildings, schools, and institutional facilities may be good candidates for solar installation.

Do you install commercial solar panels in Toronto?

Yes. Army Energy provides commercial solar installation in Toronto, the GTA, and across Ontario.

Can solar reduce commercial hydro bills?

A properly designed commercial solar system can help offset electricity usage and reduce long-term hydro expenses.

Are there Ontario solar incentives for businesses?

Incentives may be available depending on the property, project type, eligibility requirements, and current program availability.

Can commercial solar include battery storage?

Yes. Battery storage can be integrated with commercial solar systems to support resiliency, peak demand management, and smarter energy use.
