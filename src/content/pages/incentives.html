Ontario Solar Incentive FAQ
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT
COMMERCIAL SOLAR REBATES

Are there solar incentives for Ontario businesses?

Ontario businesses may have access to solar incentives, energy-efficiency rebates, tax credits, or funding programs depending on project type, timing, eligibility, and program availability.

Do I need approval before installing solar panels?

Some incentive programs require pre-approval before installation starts. Confirm program requirements before committing to a commercial solar project.

Can incentives apply to battery storage?

Battery storage may qualify under separate programs or project pathways. Eligibility depends on the specific program and system design.

Can Army Energy help with incentive applications?

Army Energy can help review project feasibility, identify relevant incentive pathways, and guide next steps for commercial solar projects.

Incentive amounts, program availability, and eligibility requirements are subject to change without notice. Always verify current program details before proceeding. Army Energy is not affiliated with the Ontario Government, Save on Energy, or any incentive program provider referenced on this page.
